Privacy Policy

Currency Rate Alert  ·  Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Overview

Currency Rate Alert ("the App") is a free Android application that tracks foreign exchange rates and sends push notifications when rates reach new highs. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for marketing purposes.

2. Data We Collect

The App collects only the minimum data needed to function:

  • FCM Device Token — a unique identifier generated by Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) used solely to deliver push notifications to your device.
  • Alert Preferences — your chosen currency pair (e.g. EUR → BDT) and which rate-high notifications you have enabled (7-day, 30-day, 3-month). These are stored in our Firebase Firestore database linked to your FCM token.
  • Notification History — a local log of the last 10 notifications you received, stored only on your device using Android's SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personally identifiable information.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To send you push notifications when currency rates reach new 7-day, 30-day, or 3-month highs.
  • To display the home screen widget with your selected currency pair's live rate.
  • To store your alert preferences so they are restored between app sessions.

Your FCM token and preferences are used exclusively for delivering the notification service. They are never used for advertising or analytics.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications) and Cloud Firestore (data storage). Firebase Privacy Policy →
  • Exchange Rate APIs — The App fetches publicly available exchange rate data from open financial data providers. No personal data is sent to these services.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your FCM token and alert preferences are stored in Firebase Firestore as long as the App is installed on your device. Uninstalling the App will cause your FCM token to become invalid; the associated Firestore record is automatically cleaned up within 30 days.

To request immediate deletion of your data, contact us at the email address below and we will remove your record from Firestore within 7 business days.

6. Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET — required to fetch live exchange rate data and connect to Firebase.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — required (Android 13+) to display rate-alert push notifications.

No other permissions (camera, contacts, location, microphone, storage) are requested.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
